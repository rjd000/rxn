The following is a partial list of our curated, legacy services for existing clients.

RXN Dialup PPP Service

RXN Dialup PPP Service is a legacy service designed to provide internet access over 56K V.90 dialup modem lines. This service can be used when no data access, other than dialup phone service, is available.

RXN Dialup UUCP Service

RXN Dialup UUCP Service is a legacy service designed to provide store and forward email access over 56K V.90 dialup modem lines. This service is typically used by nostalgic clients.

RXN Network Service

RXN Network Service provides network architecture and design services for select clients.
